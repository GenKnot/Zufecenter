# 临时教学团队图片生成记录

生成方式：Codex 内置 ImageGen（photorealistic-natural）。三张图仅作为教学团队工作场景展示，不代表或冒充任何真实教师个人。

## 英语教学团队

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide editorial image for an English faculty team section on a university language center website
Primary request: a candid English-language teaching team preparing a communicative lesson and reviewing student speaking activities together
Scene/backdrop: bright East Asian university classroom with neutral lesson cards, laptops, notebooks and a whiteboard with only blurred abstract marks
Subject: three adult educators of varied backgrounds collaborating naturally around a table, representative people only, not named or identifiable staff
Style/medium: photorealistic documentary higher-education photography, credible and understated
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape, eye-level 35mm, educators centered with usable edges for responsive cropping
Lighting/mood: soft daylight, professional, energetic, supportive
Color palette: warm wood, soft grey, restrained navy accents
Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no school emblems, no watermark, no posed faculty portrait, no implication that these are real named staff, no distorted hands or faces
```
