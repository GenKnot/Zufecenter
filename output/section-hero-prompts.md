# 栏目主图生成记录

生成方式：Codex 内置 ImageGen（photorealistic-natural）。

统一方向：真实校园纪实摄影、16:9 横幅、人物集中在右半边、左侧为网页标题留白；不出现可读文字、校徽、品牌标识和水印。

## 1. 关于中心

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide website hero image for an About the Center landing page
Primary request: a candid editorial photograph of a small language education center team planning student support and teaching work together in a modern East Asian university setting
Scene/backdrop: bright university seminar room with subtle shelves, notebooks, course folders and a glass wall
Subject: five adult educators and student-services staff in natural discussion around a table, authentic working gestures, no one posing for camera
Style/medium: photorealistic documentary university photography, natural skin and fabric texture, restrained and credible
Composition/framing: very wide 16:9 landscape; people and activity grouped mainly in the right half; generous clean negative space on the left for website title overlay; eye-level 35mm lens
Lighting/mood: soft natural daylight, calm, professional, welcoming
Color palette: neutral warm wood and soft grey with restrained navy accents
Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no school emblems, no watermark, no staged handshake, no advertising gloss, no distorted hands or faces
```
